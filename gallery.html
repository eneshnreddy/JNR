Welcome to JNR Girls PG

Welcome to JNR Girls PG, a premium paying guest accommodation exclusively for women, located in the heart of Electronic City, Bangalore. We are strategically situated near leading IT companies and educational institutions including Infosys, Wipro, TCS, IFIM College, PES University, and many other corporate offices and colleges.

Whether you're a student beginning your academic journey or a working professional building your career, JNR Girls PG offers the perfect combination of comfort, security, convenience, and affordability. Our thoughtfully designed living spaces create a peaceful environment where you can study, work, and relax with complete peace of mind.

We provide fully furnished Single Sharing, Double Sharing, and Triple Sharing Rooms, equipped with comfortable beds, wardrobes, study tables, attached bathrooms, high-speed Wi-Fi, and modern interiors. Every room is maintained to provide a clean, hygienic, and welcoming atmosphere.

Residents enjoy freshly prepared hygienic home-style meals every day along with daily housekeeping, laundry support, purified drinking water, uninterrupted power backup, and excellent maintenance services. Our dedicated staff ensures that every resident feels at home.

Safety is our highest priority. The entire premises are protected with 24×7 CCTV surveillance, biometric entry access, secure gates, and dedicated security staff, ensuring a safe and comfortable environment exclusively for women.

Located close to shopping malls, supermarkets, restaurants, hospitals, bus stops, and metro connectivity, JNR Girls PG offers everything you need within easy reach, making your everyday life simple and convenient.

Browse through our gallery and promotional video to explore our spacious rooms, modern facilities, comfortable interiors, and vibrant community. Experience the quality of living that has made JNR Girls PG a preferred choice for students and professionals alike.
